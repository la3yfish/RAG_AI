# UnitTech — Internal FAQ (Synthetic)

## Payments
**Q:** What is the standard payment term for supply contracts?  
**A:** Net 30 days after delivery, unless a contract explicitly states otherwise.

## Delivery
**Q:** When does the risk of accidental loss transfer to the client?  
**A:** At the moment of handing over the goods to the client (as stated in the supply contract template).

## Warranty
**Q:** What is the standard warranty period?  
**A:** 12 months from the delivery date, unless otherwise specified.

## Claims
**Q:** What is the target complaint (claim) rate?  
**A:** < 3% (internal KPI target).
