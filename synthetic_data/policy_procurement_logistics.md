# Procurement & Logistics Policy (Synthetic)

## 1. Purpose
Define standard rules for procurement, supplier selection, and logistics.

## 2. Currency handling
2.1. All contracts must specify a single settlement currency (e.g., RUB, CNY, USD).  
2.2. If supplier currency is not RUB, the project record must store `currency` and `budget` separately.

## 3. Customs delay risk
3.1. If a shipment has customs risk, the project status must include a risk note: `customs_delay`.  
3.2. The logistics team must provide at least two alternative routes and estimated delay ranges.

## 4. KPI target
4.1. Complaint rate target: **< 3%**.  
4.2. Repeat orders target: **>= 70%**.
